# StockLens Product Context

## What is StockLens?

StockLens is a beginner-friendly Indonesian stock market intelligence and research application.

It helps users explore Indonesian stocks through:
- current company condition
- financial history
- business health and growth
- current valuation
- historical valuation outcomes
- stock-specific historical evidence

StockLens does not provide investment recommendations.

The application presents information, analysis, historical outcomes, and comparisons.
The final investment decision remains with the user.

## Target User

The primary users are beginners who want to understand Indonesian stocks without using an intimidating professional trading terminal.

The product should make financial information easier to understand without removing important analytical context.

## Core Product Flow

Market
→ Select a stock
→ Review current condition
→ Review financial history
→ Review current valuation
→ Review historical evidence
→ User makes their own judgment

## Main Navigation

The application has only two primary navigation items:

1. Market
2. Profile

Market is the main research workspace.

Stock research is part of Market and is not a separate top-level navigation item.

## Stock Research Tabs

When a user opens a stock:

- Overview
- Financials
- Health & Growth
- Valuation
- Historical Evidence

## Market Overview

The Market Overview is the main landing page.

It shows available stocks in the local research database.

Users can:
- search stocks
- filter stocks
- sort stocks
- open a stock's research page
- add a new stock

Only stocks that have already been added to the application's database are shown in the main stock list.

## Stock Research Philosophy

StockLens separates:

### Current Condition
What the company and valuation look like now.

### Historical Evidence
How similar conditions have behaved for this specific stock in the past.

The product should not assume that one valuation method works equally well for every stock.

Historical outcomes are evidence, not guarantees.

## Valuation Philosophy

The valuation section presents estimated valuation results generated by the analysis engine.

The UI should communicate:
- current price
- estimated intrinsic value
- margin of safety
- valuation consensus
- results of individual valuation approaches when appropriate

The UI should present results clearly without exposing internal calculation formulas.

## Backtest Philosophy

The backtest compares historical stock conditions with subsequent price outcomes.

Important concepts include:
- Consensus Verdict
- MoS Verdict
- WIN
- RECOVERED
- RISK
- FLAT
- historical success rate
- sample count

Historical performance must always be presented as historical evidence, not as a prediction.

## Product Tone

StockLens should feel:
- calm
- trustworthy
- analytical
- approachable
- beginner-friendly

It should not feel:
- speculative
- aggressive
- like a trading terminal
- like a signal-selling product
